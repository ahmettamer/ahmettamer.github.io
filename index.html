I am an Electrical & Electronics Engineering graduate (B.Sc., Honor Student, GPA 3.25 / 4.00) from Abdullah Gül University, Kayseri, Turkey. My work spans embedded systems (STM32, ESP32, Raspberry Pi), FPGA design in VHDL, and machine learning for signal processing and computer vision. I am seeking an electrical, embedded or edge-AI engineering role.

Machine learning: At MTM Biotech, I developed a CNN and wavelet-transform model that decodes hand gestures from EMG signals with 82% accuracy, enabling individual finger control of a prosthetic arm. I owned the full pipeline, from data collection, preprocessing and feature engineering to model training and real-time hardware inference.

Embedded & electrical: At Four Mansions Hotel, I designed and deployed 3 remote gate openers using ESP32 and Blynk IoT, serving about 300 guests per week with no reported problems. I optimized industrial lighting infrastructure, diagnosed and repaired a major electrical fault in the lighting system of a historic building, and built a Python web-scraping tool that tracks competitor hotel pricing and occupancy trends, all in a live environment.

Graduation project & academic work: I designed AI-powered smart glasses for visually impaired users, running YOLOv6-N detection on a Raspberry Pi 5 and conveying obstacle direction and distance through real-time 3D spatial audio. Other projects include a PID line follower robot on STM32, an FPGA digital password lock, deep learning classification of breast cancer histopathological images, heart disease prediction, and a PAM digital equalization system achieving ~22 dB SNR.

Languages: Arabic (native), English (fluent) and Turkish (fluent). I am passionate about applying engineering to problems that matter, from prosthetics to accessibility technology.
